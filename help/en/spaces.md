Modules such as news, jobs or people keep their entries in **spaces**:
always one by default, more if needed, e.g. the jobs for apprentices
next to all the others. A page shows one space; which one is set in the
settings of the page under "This page shows". The sitemap marks such
pages.

Here you see each space with its page and how many entries it holds.
You can rename spaces and add new ones. A space that holds nothing and
that no page shows can be deleted.
