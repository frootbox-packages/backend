Open a page of the website while you are signed in. The **editor bar**
at the bottom switches the editing on and off.

## Change text

Click a text to change it. Rich text has a toolbar for bold, italic,
lists, links and headings. Changes are saved as you type and land in
the draft. If a change cannot be saved, for example without a
connection, this browser keeps it. The next time you open the page,
the status offers it again: **Save them now** or **Discard**.

## Arrange blocks

A page consists of blocks such as a teaser, a gallery or a quote. Use
the plus between blocks to add one, the handle to move it, the menu of
a block to copy or remove it.

## Headings

Choose a heading as large or small. The level on the page (H2, H3, …)
follows from where it is, so the outline is always right. **Outline**
in the bar shows the headings of the page.

## Undo and publish

**Undo** takes back the last change. The state in the bar tells
whether visitors see what you see; **Publish** puts the page online.

## Title, address and languages

**Page** in the bar opens the settings of the page: title, address and
the texts for search engines, with **Save** into the draft. If the
address changes, the page loads at the new one; the old one redirects
once it is published. Under **Languages** you open the version in
another language or add it, under **Visibility** you leave the page out
of search engines or the search of the website. Everything else, such
as times or locks, is in the backend. On a phone **Page** is under
**More**.

## Navigations

Pointing at a menu of the website, like the main navigation, shows its
tab with the pencil (from the keyboard **Alt+F10** at a link). It opens
the panel **Navigation**: every item can be edited, moved up or down,
indented below the item before or removed, and **Add an item** adds a
new one. Every change goes into the draft at once and shows on the
page; **Publish** puts it online with the page. A language without
items of its own shows those of another one until you add **Own
items**. All navigations are under **More** as well.

## History of a block

The clock at a block opens its history: who changed which field when,
with the old and the new text. The whole history of the page is in the
backend.
