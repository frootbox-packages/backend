**Fields of the website** are texts and images that are the same on
every page, such as the name of the website and the address in the
footer. The theme decides which there are.

## Change them

Change them right on the website, or here by language. A field left
empty in another language shows the value of the first language. Lists,
such as profiles in social networks, are changed on the website.

## Publish and go back

Changes are a draft until you **Publish** them; **Discard the
changes** keeps what visitors see. Under **Versions** you bring back an
earlier state with **Restore**. It becomes a draft first, so you can
check it before visitors see it.
