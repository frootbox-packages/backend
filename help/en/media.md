**Media** keeps images, videos and documents for your pages.

## Upload

Drag files onto the screen or choose **Upload**. Large files go up in
parts, so a weak connection does not lose them.

## Alternative text and focus point

Describe what an image shows in its **alternative text**, for people
who cannot see it and for search engines. The **focus point** keeps the
important part in view when an image is cut, e.g. on a phone.

You set both where you see the image: on the website, the label of an
image has the buttons "Alternative text" and "Set the focus point".
Click where the image should stay in view. The focus point belongs to
the image and counts at every place that uses it; visitors see it once
you publish.

## Made with AI

Choose under **AI** whether an AI generated or edited an image. The
website then shows a small badge “AI” on it, wherever it is used. Many
tools write this into the file; it is set when you upload such a file.
On a page already published, the badge comes once you publish the
change.

## Folders and tags

Folders and tags help to find files again. A file can be used on many
pages; **Replace the file** changes it everywhere at once.

## Unused files

The filter **Only unused** shows files that no page uses, not even in a
draft. They can go.
