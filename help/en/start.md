The backend is where you look after your website. The menu on the left
leads to everything you may use; what you may not use is not shown.

## Find anything

The search at the top of the menu finds pages, media, users and screens
by their words, drafts included. Press **Ctrl K**, or **⌘ K** on a Mac,
to open it from anywhere. Choose a hit with the arrow keys and open it
with Enter.

## Edit on the website

Most changes happen on the website itself: open a page and switch on
**Edit** in the editor bar at the bottom. See [Editing on the
website](editing.md).

## Nothing is lost

Every change lands in a draft first. Visitors see it only after you
publish it, and almost every action can be undone.
