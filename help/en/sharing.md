A **preview link** shows the website with all drafts to someone without
an account, e.g. a customer. Create one in the editor bar under
**More options** with **Share a preview …**; it ends after the days you
choose.

## Comments

Who has a preview link can comment on any place of the website. You see
the comments in the editing mode at the same place and on the screen
**Comments**; answer them and mark them done.

## Password protection

Under **Access** the whole website can be protected with a password,
e.g. before it goes live.
