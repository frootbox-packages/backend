**Design** changes the look of the whole website within what the theme
allows: colors, fonts, spaces and the **fields of the website** such as
the name and address in the footer.

## Try first

Changes to the design are a draft as well. Open **All elements** to see
every block of the theme with the new design before visitors do, then
**Publish the design**. **Discard the changes** returns to the
published design.
