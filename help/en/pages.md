The **Sitemap** on the left shows all pages as a tree. Click a page to
see its details on the right: title, address, layout and state.

## Create a page

Choose **New page**, give it a title and pick where it goes. The address
follows from the title, you can change it later. A new page is a draft:
visitors do not see it yet.

## Move a page

Drag a page in the Sitemap, or use the keyboard: with **Alt** and the
arrow keys a page moves among its siblings, into the page above it or
one level up. The addresses of the page and its children follow, old
addresses redirect to the new ones.

## Publish and take offline

**Publish** puts the draft online. **Take offline** hides a page from
visitors without deleting it. **Discard** throws away the draft and
keeps the published version.

## Lock a page

A locked page stays online for editors but visitors get a 404, e.g.
for a page that is not ready yet.

## Protect a page with a password

Under **Visibility** a page gets a password, if you like also for its
subpages. Visitors then only see a password field; whoever knows the
password gets in for 14 days. Signed-in users and preview links do not
need it. The protection works right away, also before publishing.
Search engines, the sitemap and the search of the website leave the
page out; it stays in navigations. A **new password** locks out
everyone who got in with the old one. The password does not protect
the images and files of the page.

## History

**History** shows who changed the page when, through which channel and
on which device, with the old and the new text. Publishing, discarding
and the scheduler are in it too, so a text that is gone can be traced:
removed, never published or discarded. **Whole history** shows all
changes of a year, by language, also after they can no longer be
undone.
