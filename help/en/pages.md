The **Sitemap** on the left shows all pages as a tree. Click a page to
see its details on the right: title, address, layout and state.

## Create a page

Choose **New page**, give it a title and pick where it goes. The address
follows from the title, you can change it later. A new page is a draft:
visitors do not see it yet.

## Move a page

Drag a page in the Sitemap, or use the keyboard: with **Alt** and the
arrow keys a page moves among its siblings, into the page above it or
one level up. The addresses of the page and its children follow, old
addresses redirect to the new ones.

## Publish and take offline

**Publish** puts the draft online. **Take offline** hides a page from
visitors without deleting it. **Discard** throws away the draft and
keeps the published version.

## Lock a page

A locked page stays online for editors but visitors get a 404, e.g.
for a page that is not ready yet.
